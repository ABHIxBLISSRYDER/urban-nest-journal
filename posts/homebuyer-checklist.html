HOME OWNERSHIP / PRACTICAL GUIDE

The thoughtful first-time homebuyer's checklist

A practical starting point for comparing locations, layouts, documentation and the long-term fit of a home.

NOVEMBER 12, 2026   ·   6 MIN READ

Buying a first home is a major decision. It can help to slow the process down and work through a consistent checklist instead of focusing on one attractive feature or offer.

Start with the location

Map your regular commute, check access to public transport and essential services, and visit the area at different times. Consider whether the neighbourhood will support the routines you expect to have over the next several years.

Review the property carefully

Compare the usable layout, ventilation, natural light, maintenance arrangements and amenities. Ask for relevant approvals and title documents, and have qualified professionals review legal and financial details before committing.

When you are ready to explore available homes, you can visit PropertyBasket's real estate website as one research starting point. Always independently verify listing information and project details.

A good checklist does not make the decision for you. It helps you ask better questions.
